A calmer command center for teams making decisions with live, fragmented information.

UX strategyInformation architectureUI design
BEHIND THE INTERFACE / 01

The thinking
behind the pixels.

01 / CONTEXT

In this concept brief, an operations team must move between maps, alerts, and status feeds to understand what changed. The interface needs to support fast orientation without hiding detail.

02 / CORE UX DECISION

Lead with a concise now / next view. Group signals by urgency and relationship, then let people drill into a place, event, or source only when they need the full picture.

03 / UI RESPONSE

A spatial canvas anchors the experience. A contextual side rail carries the active decision, while restrained color is reserved for changes that actually require attention.

04 / WHAT I'D TEST

Can someone identify the most important change and its next action within the first few seconds, even when several alerts arrive together?

Helping people understand what their money can do next, not just what it did yesterday.

Product thinkingInteraction designVisual systems
BEHIND THE INTERFACE / 02

The thinking
behind the pixels.

01 / CONTEXT

Balances alone can feel abstract. In this concept brief, people need to see how upcoming commitments affect the choices they can make today.

02 / CORE UX DECISION

Organize the home view around upcoming commitments and room to act. Every forecast should be inspectable, with a plain explanation of the assumptions behind it.

03 / UI RESPONSE

Use a quiet hierarchy, clear time frames, and accessible chart labels. The interface treats uncertainty as useful information instead of presenting a false sense of precision.

04 / WHAT I'D TEST

After viewing a projection, can someone explain why it changed and confidently adjust an assumption without feeling penalized?

CASE STUDY / 03CONCEPT EXPLORATION — 2026
Public services
03
Public services

Openline✳

A more legible path through eligibility, documents, and multi-step applications.

Service designAccessible UXDesign systems
BEHIND THE INTERFACE / 03

The thinking
behind the pixels.

01 / CONTEXT

A public service application can ask for significant time and personal information. People deserve to know the requirements before they begin and where they are in the process.

02 / CORE UX DECISION

Show eligibility and document needs up front. Break the task into meaningful steps, with a persistent summary and clear ways to recover from an interruption.

03 / UI RESPONSE

Plain-language prompts, generous target sizes, and unmistakable progress cues create an interface that works across devices and different levels of digital confidence.

04 / WHAT I'D TEST

Can a first-time applicant predict the next step, pause safely, and return without repeating work or losing context?
